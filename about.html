About GamerPlayground & Organisational History

Our History

GamerPlayground started in 2026 as a passionate community of gaming purists. We noticed a troubling trend: the digital shift was erasing ownership. Digital storefronts frequently delist titles, leaving players with empty digital shelves. What began as a local physical game trading club quickly evolved into a dedicated mission to preserve physical media. Today, we are transitioning from a community hub into a premier online marketplace to ensure physical discs remain accessible to everyone.

Mission Statement

To preserve gaming history and guarantee true ownership by providing gamers with reliable access to physical game discs.

Vision Statement

To become the world's most trusted sanctuary for physical media, where games are collected, owned, and cherished forever.

Target Audience Breakdown

  • Physical Media Purists: Collectors who demand tangible ownership, box art, and physical discs.
  • Preservationists: Gamers worried about digital delisting and the loss of gaming history.
  • Gift Buyers: Parents and friends who prefer gifting a physical, tangible item over a digital code.
  • Budget-Conscious Players: Gamers who rely on buying, selling, and trading used physical discs to fund their hobby.
