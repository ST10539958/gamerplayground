## 1. Project Overview & Business Proposal

### Organisation Name
**GamerPlayground**

### History of the Organisation
GamerPlayground started in 2026 as a passionate community of gaming purists responding to the rapid digital shift in the interactive media landscape. Witnessing digital storefronts systematically delisting classic titles and revoking user purchase licenses, the founders started a localized physical game trading club. To scale this preservation movement, GamerPlayground is evolving into a dedicated online consumer marketplace to keep tangible hard discs accessible worldwide.

### Mission Statement
To preserve gaming history and guarantee true media ownership by providing collectors and gamers with reliable access to physical game discs.

### Vision Statement
To become the world's most trusted sanctuary for physical media, where games are collected, owned, and cherished forever.

### Target Audience Breakdown
*   **Physical Media Purists:** Collectors demanding physical game boxes, original insert booklets, and high-shelf display values.
*   **Preservationists:** Players concerned with server closures, digital delisting trends, and long-term history archival.
*   **Gift Buyers:** Consumers prioritizing tangible, physically wrapped boxes for holiday gifts over abstract digital voucher codes.
*   **Budget-Conscious Players:** Gamers relying on buying, testing, selling, and trading down physical game collections to fund their hobby.

## 2. Platform Strategy & Performance Tracking

### Website Goals
1.  **Exclusive Marketplace:** Establish a reliable portal entirely clear of digital download keys or ongoing internet verification check-ins.
2.  **Grading Clarity:** Provide complete transparency over case conditions, surface scratches, and regional data (PAL/NTSC).
3.  **Community Integration:** Connect collectors securely through user-driven trade-in valuation streams.

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
*   **Sales Conversion Rate:** Tracking the percentage of buyers checking out actual physical games.
*   **Bounce Rate:** Reviewing product data card clarity based on how long users spend viewing disc condition assessments.
*   **Trade Submissions Volume:** Measuring the frequency of incoming trade-in appraisal forms submitted per week.

## 3. Technical Requirements & Design Execution

### Design Scope Constraint
Per the specific project constraints, this system has been constructed using **pure HTML only**.
*   **No CSS:** Relies strictly on native browser rendering, HTML boundary lines (`<hr>`), and layout tables to format alignment.
*   **No JavaScript:** Relies entirely on native HTML form processing actions and direct standard file hyper-linking (`href`).

### Sitemaps & Page Inventory
The platform structure consists of the following modular files, linked horizontally inside the semantic `<header>` row:
1.  `index.html` (Homepage) - Hero message, value pillars, and trending stock overview tables.
2.  `about.html` (About Us) - Extensive organizational history and company manifestos.
3.  `products.html` (Product Catalog) - Search filter tables and console platform breakdown arrays.
4.  `contact.html` (Contact Us) - Support inquiry structures, message fields, and distribution hub location markers.
5.  `community.html` (Community and Trade-ins) - Trade rules and a dynamic interactive-ready manual submission intake form.
